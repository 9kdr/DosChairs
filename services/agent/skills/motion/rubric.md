# Motion rubric

Score the module 0-2 per criterion. Ship at 8 or above.

1. **Feel.** Momentum in, overshoot and settle; staggered entrances; one idea per beat. Nothing linear, nothing flashy.
2. **Determinism.** Re-simulated from 0..t at a fixed step; identical frames after seeking away and back.
3. **Taste.** Restrained and editorial per `taste.md`. Idle motion (float, grain) stays faint.
4. **Controls.** Spring stiffness/damping, stagger step, and tempo are Inspector numbers with sane ranges; every one visibly changes the frame.
5. **Seekability.** No running timelines: paused libraries plus a render-pass twin for DOM-only drivers. Backwards scrubs replay from a checkpoint.
