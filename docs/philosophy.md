# Product philosophy: never destructive, always undoable

Chair applies its edits directly, on copies — never on the source.

- A **changeset** is an applied edit. A **timeline** is the cut. An **adapter** is the link to one editing app.
- Every apply lands on a copy: a new timeline, a new comp version, a new track. Never overwrite source video or audio, and never mutate the person's existing timeline in place.
- History shows every step with who did it. Undo is one key.
- The app shows its work: the thread, the activity, and the exact diff.

New issues and pull requests can cite this note instead of re-arguing it.
